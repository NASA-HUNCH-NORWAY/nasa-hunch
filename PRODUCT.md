## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences with equal weight (confirmed 2026-10-20):

- **Teachers and school leaders** in Norwegian videregående skole, mainly Teknologi- og industrifag, but also Teknologi og forskningslære, Restaurant og matfag and Informasjonsteknologi. They are judging whether HUNCH fits their subjects, what it costs the school and what starting involves. Success: they get in touch.
- **Financial and professional partners** (foundations, industry, workshops). They are judging whether the organisation is credible and what their support would pay for. Success: they get in touch about support.

## Product Purpose

NASA HUNCH Norge is an ideell forening that runs NASA's HUNCH programme (High School Students United with NASA to Create Hardware) in Norway. Students build and develop hardware to real NASA needs and specifications, either production to drawings or design and prototyping. Participation is free for the school. The website explains the programme, recruits schools and recruits supporting partners.

## Positioning

Norway is the first country outside the USA to run HUNCH. Norwegian vocational students produce parts to NASA drawings and deliver them to NASA. No other Norwegian school programme gives students work to a real space customer's specification.

## Operating Context

The school year runs: start-up with NASA HUNCH from the USA in the autumn, first design review in November, critical design review in February before production, and delivery of finished products in the spring. The work happens in school workshops: drawings, tolerances, measurement, documentation and testing are part of the assignment.

## Product Principles

1. Show the real work: drawings, parts, tolerances and deliveries are the evidence, not abstract space imagery.
2. Give schools and partners equal, clear paths. Each page has one primary action.
3. Say only what is true and confirmed. A missing photo or number stays a marked placeholder until it exists.
4. Free for schools is a fact to show early, not a footnote.
